What we ask of the board

  • Approve emergency changes for the 2 root causes rated Act (RC-002, RC-005): actively exploited and exposed or safety-relevant. Together ₹3.1 crore a year of expected loss.
  • Back the plant schedule: 2 of the top 10 fixes are on OT and need OEM approval and a planned outage. Compensating controls (virtual patch, segmentation) hold the risk until then, and the CEA clock should show that state, not a red mark.
  • Close the blind spots: 12 registered assets are never scanned, including 4 crown-jewel systems and 8 OT devices. Their risk is not in the figure above.
Every number has a receipt. Each ID above lists its findings, hosts, scanner references (QID or plugin) and source-file rows in receipts.json; findings.csv and root_causes.csv hold the full detail. Duplicates across Qualys and Tenable are merged (389 findings seen by both). Rupee risk follows FAIR (loss event frequency × loss magnitude) with 3,000 Monte Carlo runs; frequency uses exploitation state and network zone, magnitude uses each business process's ₹ per hour of downtime from the CMDB plus response, data-breach and OT costs. Every assumption is in sutra/risk.py and is a hypothesis, not calibrated loss data. SSVC is a simplified deployer tree. CEA windows come from a vendor summary and must be checked against the gazette text.
